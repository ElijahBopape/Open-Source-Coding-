## Short description (max 80 characters)
Modules, timetable, tasks, exams and a smart study planner - in one app.

## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Student Assistant brings everything a tertiary student needs to stay on top of their studies into one simple app. You no longer need a separate calendar, to-do app and notes app.

**See what matters today**
Your dashboard shows your next class, your next exam with a countdown, your average mark and today's study sessions. It also lists the tasks that need your attention first.

**Academic Priority System**
Tasks are ordered by priority and due date, not alphabetically. When a module has an exam coming up within a week, its tasks automatically move up in priority.

**Smart study planner**
Pick an exam or an assignment, and Smart plan spreads your revision sessions evenly over the days you have left. Tick sessions off and watch your progress grow.

**Exams and marks**
Keep track of exam dates, venues and times. After you write an exam, add your mark to see your average.

**Works offline**
Add, edit and complete tasks with no signal. Your changes sync automatically when you're back online.

**Reminders that arrive on time**
Get push notifications for new tasks and exams, and daily reminders for work due today or tomorrow.

**Your language**
Use the app in English, Sepedi or isiZulu.

**Sign in your way**
Sign in with Google, or with your email and password. Your settings follow you to any phone.
