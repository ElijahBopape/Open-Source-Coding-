# Student Assistant — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

Student Assistant ("the app") is a student project by Elijah Bopape (ST10445847) for
OPSC6312 at The Independent Institute of Education.

## What we collect
- **Account details:** your name, your email address and, if you choose to add one, a profile photo. We handle sign-in through Firebase Authentication. **We never see or store your password.**
- **Your study data:** the modules, tasks, timetable entries, exams, marks and study sessions you enter.
- **Settings:** your language, theme and notification preference.
- **Device token:** a Firebase Cloud Messaging token, used only to send you push notifications.

## How it is used
Your data is used only to provide the app's features: syncing your data between devices, reminders and notifications. It is never sold, shared for advertising, or used to track you.

## Where it is stored
- Account details: Google Firebase Authentication.
- Study data and settings: Google Cloud Firestore, accessed through the app's own API hosted on Render.
- Profile photos: Cloudinary.
- A copy of your tasks is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, so the app works offline.

All network traffic uses HTTPS.

## Your choices
- You can turn notifications off in **Settings**.
- Signing out removes your data from the phone.
- To delete your account and all of your data, email **bopapeelijah09@gmail.com**. We will delete it within 30 days.

## Children
The app is intended for tertiary (post-school) students and is not directed at children under 13.
